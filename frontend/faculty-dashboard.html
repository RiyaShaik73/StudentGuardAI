How the Student Success Score Works

Each student's score is calculated from five indicators. These are prototype weights and have not been validated as a prediction model.

Indicator Weight What it tells us
Attendance 25% How regularly the student attends classes
Assignment Completion 15% How consistently assignments are completed
Engagement 10% Student participation and engagement indicators
Technical Skills 20% Current technical skill level
Placement Readiness 30% Preparedness for placement opportunities
